# WASM-4 credits

120 games for [WASM-4](https://wasm4.org), a fantasy console for tiny retro games made with WebAssembly. They come from WASM-4's own cart archive ([aduros/wasm4 `site/static/carts`](https://github.com/aduros/wasm4/tree/9d6c962785cfe3719d0245fd279ecbe98a4dbb63/site/static/carts)), where every cart is licensed under **[Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)**: free to share and adapt with credit, **non-commercial only**, and adaptations must keep the same license.

Each game is the original cart, unchanged, bundled with the WASM-4 web player (© Bruno Garcia, ISC license) into one standalone `index.html` using WASM-4's own `w4 bundle` tool. Each folder's `LICENSE.txt` has the credit and both licenses.

Left out: tools, tech demos and tests (sound/palette/particle tools, input examples and similar), and games that copy commercial games or characters (Tetris and Puyo Puyo clones, a Space Invaders remake, Touhou, Undertale and Wolfenstein fan games, a Plants vs Zombies parody, a Minicraft port, a remake of another indie game).
